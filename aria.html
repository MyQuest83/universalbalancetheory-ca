From an idea to a test

  1. Define the questionState one claim, the system it concerns, and the limits of the test.
  2. Name the variablesRecord what each variable means, its units, source, and measurement limits.
  3. Choose controls before resultsCompare with ordinary baselines. When the formula is relevant, retain the ordinary-pi control.
  4. Keep a reproducible recordSave the method, source versions, raw outcomes, uncertainty, and any deviations.

Keep the claim within the evidence.

Separate what was observed, what was calculated, and what you infer. A missing test remains missing; an uncertain result remains uncertain.

AI can help organize a test, but the participant is responsible for checking sources and results. A calculation or a successful simulation alone does not establish new physics.
