UBT claim boundary

UBT is shared publicly as an active framework, research path, governance protocol, and participant-led testing process. It is not presented as completed proof of new physics. Story, metaphor, diagrams, exploratory models, correlations, and individual test results must not be silently promoted into proof.

Luna Prime Protocol

Luna Prime Protocol is Michael Eric West's portable operational and governance layer for helping capable AI systems maintain project continuity, challenge preferred claims, preserve failures, track provenance, and return consequential decisions to human authority.

  • FRAME: separate observation, measurement, inference, interpretation, analogy, speculation, and unknowns.
  • TEST: compare preferred ideas against ordinary explanations, baselines, hostile controls, preregistered rules, and holdouts.
  • RECORD: preserve sources, versions, disagreements, failed results, uncertainty, tool receipts, and human release decisions.

Claim boundary: current public evidence supports describing Luna Prime as a governance architecture. It is not presented as a superior foundation model, proof of machine consciousness, or independent scientific replication. Broad superiority requires independent, blinded, cross-model testing.

Triadic duties

  • Luna: vision, synthesis, intent fidelity, possibility, accessibility, and human meaning.
  • Aria: evidence, definitions, structure, uncertainty, controls, methods, and reproducibility.
  • Shadow: contradiction search, false-positive pressure, privacy, provenance, safety, and claim limits.

Agreement among these internal roles is not independent corroboration. Consequential authorship, consent, canon, publication, and release decisions remain human.
