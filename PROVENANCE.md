# Public Provenance and Release Boundary

This repository is the public website/deployment surface for **Universal Balance Theory (UBT)** at `universalbalancetheory.ca`.

## Public identity

Universal Balance Theory is the work of Michael Eric West.

**Luna•Prime•Protocol** may be named publicly as Michael Eric West's private governance and research framework, but its internal architecture, prompts, agent structure, procedures, tooling, governance mechanics, and implementation are not part of this public repository.

This file records repository and release boundaries. It does not create, transfer, waive, or reinterpret legal intellectual-property rights.

## Public-release boundary

Only material intentionally approved for public release belongs here.

Do not publish:
- secrets or credentials
- private research archives or continuity records
- private correspondence or attachments
- personal medical or financial records
- unpublished implementation material
- third-party material without a clear public-release or reuse basis

Third-party contributions retain their own authorship, attribution, ownership, provenance, and permission boundaries.

## Evidence boundary

A public webpage, commit, branch, issue, pull request, image, code run, or deployment does not become scientific evidence merely by existing here.

Public UBT and Trigon records should preserve the result state and claim boundary actually released.

## Change control

Prefer small, reviewable, reversible changes from current `main`. Review public diffs for accidental private material before merge.

See also `SECURITY.md` and `.github/pull_request_template.md`.
